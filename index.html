Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

I am Aberham Melaku, a professional Video Editor, Colorist, and Motion Designer.

I am a professional Video Editor, Colorist, and Motion Designer with experience creating high-quality visual content for leading Ethiopian brands and companies.

Through my work with Inframe Production, I have contributed to projects for brands such as Ethio Telecom (Zemen Gebeya campaign), TECNO Camon 50, and Totot Cultural Restaurant, producing social media advertisements, event promotions, and commercial content.

I have also worked on various YouTube productions, podcasts, and events, collaborating with talented colorists, cinematographers, and motion designers in the Ethiopian creative industry.

My expertise includes professional video editing, color grading, motion design, and visual storytelling, using Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve. My passion is transforming creative ideas into powerful visual experiences through editing, motion, and color.
